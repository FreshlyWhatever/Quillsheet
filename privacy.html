Quillsheet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

Quillsheet does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no advertising and no server. Your characters stay in your own browser, and nothing leaves it unless you export a file yourself.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Quillsheet is a set of static files (HTML, CSS and JavaScript) that runs entirely in your web browser. The app never sends what you type, paste or import to us or to anyone else.

The page includes a Content Security Policy with connect-src 'none', which tells your browser to block the app from sending anything over the network. You can confirm this yourself in your browser's developer tools (Network tab). The only downloads are Quillsheet's own files, from the same address you opened it at, so it can run and keep an offline copy of itself.

2. Where your characters are stored

  • Browser storage (localStorage): your character sheets, your homebrew library and small preferences (such as the last character you opened, theme, text size and dice settings) are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. Only pages from the same site address can read it.
  • Offline copy (service worker and cache): so Quillsheet opens with no internet, your browser keeps a copy of the site's own files (pages, styles, code and rules content) on this device. It contains none of your characters or anything you type.
  • Files you export: when you choose "Download file" or "Back up all characters", a file is saved to your device. What happens to it afterwards (for example, emailing it to your DM) is up to you.
  • Encrypted exports: if you choose an encrypted export, the file is locked with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 250,000 iterations). The passphrase is never stored. If you forget it, the file cannot be recovered.

3. Importing homebrew

The Import Homebrew feature reads text or JSON that you copy and paste, or a file you choose. It understands common community formats, such as the JSON used by 5etools. Quillsheet does not connect to 5etools, any official Wizards of the Coast website, or any other site, and those sites are not told that you use Quillsheet. Content you copy from elsewhere stays covered by its own terms and licences.

4. Hosting

If you use a copy of Quillsheet hosted on a web server, that server may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, time and requested file), as every web server does. Those logs come from the hosting setup, not from the app, and they never include character data, because that data never leaves your browser. Whoever hosts the copy should say how long they keep those logs. Opening Quillsheet from files on your own computer involves no server at all.

5. Deleting your data

  • Delete a single character with the 🗑 button on its sheet.
  • Delete everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings ("Cookies and site data" or "Website data").

Because we never had your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children

Quillsheet collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

7. About this project

Quillsheet is a non-commercial passion project, made to help busy people spend less time on bookkeeping and more time on the fun (and genuinely hard) parts of D&D: playing the character, making choices and telling the story together.

Its only goal is a better character-sheet experience. Keeping everything on your device keeps it fast, makes it work offline once loaded, and means there is no account to break into and no data to leak.

Quillsheet is unofficial fan content. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast or 5etools. Dungeons & Dragons is a trademark of Wizards of the Coast LLC.
